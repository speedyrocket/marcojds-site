02About

I'm a Senior IT Manager / Cloud Product Engineer at Procter & Gamble by day, and a serial side-project starter by night. My background is actually in Manufacturing Engineering (Mechatronics & Robotics) from De La Salle University — I kind of fell into cloud and software along the way, and never left.

Some highlights: co-founded a food delivery startup during the pandemic, made it to the finals of the NASA International Space Apps Challenge, and once won gold at an international robotics olympiad as a teenager. My favorite thing to do is learn new things, mostly to discover how much more there is to learn.
