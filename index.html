02About

Hey, I'm Marco 👋 By day I keep cloud infrastructure running for Procter & Gamble. By night — and most weekends — I'm usually elbow-deep in some random side project, most of which never see daylight. This site is one of the ones that did.

I actually started out studying Manufacturing Engineering with a focus on robotics, which is a roundabout way of saying I have no formal software degree — I just kept building things until "full-stack engineer" fit better than whatever my job title says.

I'm the kind of person who'll happily go down a rabbit hole learning something completely unrelated to what I'm supposed to be doing, just because it seemed interesting. This site — and whatever ends up in the projects section — is mostly a byproduct of that.
