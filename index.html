02About

By trade I'm a Senior IT Manager / Cloud Product Engineer at Procter & Gamble. I actually started out in Manufacturing Engineering, specializing in Mechatronics & Robotics — but over the years I kept picking up more of the stack: cloud infrastructure, automation, backend, frontend. At this point I think of myself less as "the robotics guy who does IT" and more as a full-stack engineer who happens to have a robotics background.

Outside the day job I'm a serial side-project starter — I co-founded a food delivery startup during the pandemic and made it to the finals of the NASA International Space Apps Challenge. My favorite thing to do is learn new things, mostly to discover how much more there is to learn.
